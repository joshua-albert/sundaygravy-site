How the packages work

Menu shoots are the big one. We go through your menu ahead of time and make a shot list. On the day, your kitchen fires one plate at a time and I shoot it while it's fresh. Half a day gets you 20 finished photos. A full day gets you 45, with time for the dining room, the bar and your team.

Dish drops are for one-offs. A new special, a seasonal cocktail, the dessert you just added. I come in for an hour and you get six photos.

Monthly content is one visit a month and ten photos for social. It keeps your feed fed without you having to think about it.
