Philadelphia cocktail photographer

Cocktail and drink photography in Philadelphia

Cocktail lists, pours, coffee and the bar itself, for bars, restaurants and cafés.

Drinks are harder to shoot than food. Ice melts, bubbles die, glass shows every fingerprint. The trick is to shoot fast and shoot it fresh.

What I shoot for bars

  • The cocktail list, one drink at a time, so every drink matches.
  • Pours, shakes and garnishes, with your bartender's hands in frame.
  • Beer, wine and spritzes for the menu and the specials board.
  • The bar itself, set up and ready, before the room fills.

Coffee and café drinks

Latte art doesn't last long. Neither does foam. I shoot coffee the same way as cocktails: made right, shot right away, one at a time.

How a drink shoot works

We pick the drinks ahead of time. On the day your bartender builds each one fresh, I shoot it while the ice is still clear, and we move to the next. Clear drinks get light from behind so they glow. Colored ones get hard side light so the color reads.
