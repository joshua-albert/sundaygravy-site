Philadelphia food photographer

Food photographer in Philadelphia

For restaurants, bakeries and cafés that want their food to look as good as it tastes.

Hard light, bright color, and food that looks like you could reach in and grab it.

That's the style. Sharp shadows, real texture, color that pops on a phone screen. It's built for the places your food actually gets seen: Instagram, Google, the delivery apps, your menu.

Food photography for restaurants

Full menus, new dishes, specials. Shot at your place, plated by your kitchen, one dish at a time while it's fresh. If you run a restaurant, the restaurant photography page has the whole shoot day laid out.

Bakery and café photography

Bakeries and cafés need different things. The pastry case in the morning. A cross-section of the croissant. Breakfast from above with the coffee in frame. Cakes on a table outside. I come in early, while it's all fresh out of the oven.
