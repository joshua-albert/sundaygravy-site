About Sunday Gravy Studio

I'm Joshua Albert. I'm a photographer, and I spent 22 years working in restaurants.

So I know what a menu shoot has to do, and how to get it done without getting in the kitchen's way.

Sunday Gravy is a food and drink photo studio in South Philly. I shoot for restaurants, bars, bakeries and cafés. New menus, cocktail lists, openings, and the photos you need to post every week.

I like hard light and bright color. Food that looks like you could reach in and grab it. I shoot every job myself.

Sunday Gravy is the sister company of Cowdog Studio, where I shoot family and engagement portraits.
