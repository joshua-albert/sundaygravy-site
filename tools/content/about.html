About Sunday Gravy Studio

I'm Joshua Albert. I'm a photographer, and I spent 22 years working in restaurants.

I know what a menu photo has to do. I know what a kitchen looks like an hour before doors open, and I know nobody has time for a shoot that drags.

So I keep it simple. One corner of the room, one plate at a time, back to the pass while it's still hot.

Sunday Gravy is the sister studio of Cowdog Studio, where I shoot family and engagement portraits. Based in South Philly.
