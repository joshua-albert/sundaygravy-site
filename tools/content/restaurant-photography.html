Sunday Gravy is based in South Philly. I shoot for restaurants in East Passyunk, Fishtown, Center City, Old City, Northern Liberties and out on the Main Line.

What I shoot

Menu shoots

Every dish, shot so they all match. For your printed menu, website, Google listing and delivery apps. Half a day covers the dishes that sell. A full day covers the whole menu, plus the room, the bar and your team.

New openings

You need photos before you open, not after. Food, drinks, the dining room set and empty, the bar, the sign out front, the people. Everything press and your launch posts will ask for. Here's a checklist for opening photos.

Dish drops

One hour, six photos. For a new special, a seasonal dish, or the thing you just added and need on Instagram by Friday.

Monthly content

One visit a month, ten photos for social. Your feed stays fresh and you don't have to shoot it on your phone between tickets.

How a shoot day works

Before. Send me the menu. We pick the dishes, make a shot list and choose a time, usually before service. You'll get a short prep checklist so the kitchen knows what's coming.

On the day. I set up in a corner of the dining room. The kitchen fires one plate at a time. I shoot it while it's hot and send it back. If a dish needs a second try, we fire it again.

After. I pick the best frames, edit them and deliver them sized for your menu, website, social and the delivery apps.
